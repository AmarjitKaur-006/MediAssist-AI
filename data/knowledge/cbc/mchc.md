# Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration (MCHC)

## Test Name
Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration

## Aliases
MCHC

## Category
Complete Blood Count (CBC) - Red Blood Cell Indices

## What Does This Test Measure?

MCHC measures how concentrated the hemoglobin is within red blood cells.

It is a red blood cell index reported as part of a CBC.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

MCHC can be used with other red blood cell indices to help evaluate different types of anemia and other blood disorders.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

A low MCHC means that the hemoglobin concentration within red blood cells is lower than usual.

It may be associated with iron deficiency anemia or thalassemia.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

A high MCHC means that hemoglobin is more concentrated within red blood cells than usual.

It may occur in certain conditions such as hemolytic anemia or hereditary spherocytosis.

## Important Interpretation Factors

MCHC should be interpreted together with MCV, MCH, RDW, hemoglobin, hematocrit and RBC count.

The laboratory reference range should be used when determining whether the result is low, normal or high.

## Important Limitations

MCHC alone cannot establish the cause of anemia or diagnose a specific disorder.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal MCHC result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when it occurs together with other abnormal CBC results.

## Related Tests / Context

MCV, MCH, RDW, hemoglobin, hematocrit and RBC count.
