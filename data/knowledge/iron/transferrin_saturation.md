# Transferrin Saturation

## Test Name
Transferrin Saturation

## Aliases
TSAT, Transferrin Saturation

## Category
Iron Studies

## What Does This Test Measure?

Transferrin saturation estimates the percentage of transferrin's iron-binding capacity that is occupied by iron.

It is calculated using serum iron and TIBC or related measurements.

## Why Is This Test Used?

Transferrin saturation may help evaluate:

- Iron deficiency
- Iron overload
- Disorders of iron metabolism

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low transferrin saturation may be associated with insufficient circulating iron and can occur with iron deficiency.

It may also occur in some inflammatory or chronic conditions.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High transferrin saturation may indicate an increased amount of circulating iron relative to iron-binding capacity.

It can occur in some iron-overload conditions.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Transferrin saturation should be interpreted together with ferritin, serum iron and TIBC or transferrin.

## Important Limitations

Transferrin saturation alone cannot determine the underlying cause of an abnormal iron status.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal transferrin saturation should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when other iron studies are also abnormal.

## Related Tests / Context

Ferritin, serum iron, TIBC and transferrin.
