# Transferrin

## Test Name
Transferrin

## Aliases
Serum Transferrin, Transferrin Blood Test

## Category
Iron Studies

## What Does This Test Measure?

Transferrin is a blood protein that transports iron through the bloodstream.

The transferrin test measures the amount of transferrin in the blood.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Transferrin testing may be used as part of an iron study when evaluating iron deficiency, anemia or disorders involving iron metabolism.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low transferrin may be associated with:

- Inflammation
- Liver disease
- Malnutrition
- Iron overload
- Certain other conditions

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High transferrin may occur with iron deficiency.

The result should be interpreted together with serum iron and other iron studies.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Transferrin is produced primarily by the liver and can be influenced by nutritional status and inflammation.

## Important Limitations

Transferrin alone cannot determine the cause of an abnormal iron result.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal transferrin result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when ferritin or serum iron is also abnormal.

## Related Tests / Context

Serum iron, ferritin, TIBC and transferrin saturation.
