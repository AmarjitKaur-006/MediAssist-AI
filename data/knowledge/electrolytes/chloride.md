# Chloride

## Test Name
Chloride

## Aliases
Cl, Serum Chloride

## Category
Electrolytes

## What Does This Test Measure?

The chloride blood test measures the amount of chloride in the blood.

Chloride is an electrolyte that helps maintain fluid balance and acid-base balance.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Chloride is commonly measured as part of an electrolyte panel or metabolic panel.

It may help evaluate:

- Fluid balance
- Acid-base balance
- Kidney function
- Certain metabolic conditions

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low chloride, called hypochloremia, may occur with:

- Prolonged vomiting
- Certain medicines
- Fluid abnormalities
- Some metabolic conditions

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High chloride, called hyperchloremia, may occur with:

- Dehydration
- Certain kidney conditions
- Acid-base disturbances
- Excess chloride relative to body water

## Important Interpretation Factors

Chloride is commonly interpreted together with sodium, bicarbonate and other electrolytes.

## Important Limitations

An abnormal chloride value is nonspecific and cannot determine the underlying cause by itself.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal chloride result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when other electrolytes are also abnormal.

## Related Tests / Context

Sodium, potassium, bicarbonate, creatinine and BUN.
