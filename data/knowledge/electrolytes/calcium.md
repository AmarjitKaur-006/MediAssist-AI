# Calcium

## Test Name
Calcium

## Aliases
Serum Calcium, Total Calcium, Ca

## Category
Electrolytes / Mineral

## What Does This Test Measure?

The calcium blood test measures the amount of calcium in the blood.

Calcium is important for bones and teeth, muscle function, nerve signaling and normal blood clotting.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Calcium testing may be used to evaluate:

- Bone health
- Parathyroid function
- Kidney-related conditions
- Disorders affecting calcium metabolism

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low blood calcium is called hypocalcemia.

Possible associations include:

- Vitamin D deficiency
- Low parathyroid hormone activity
- Kidney disease
- Low magnesium
- Certain medicines
- Other metabolic conditions

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High blood calcium is called hypercalcemia.

Possible associations include:

- Hyperparathyroidism
- Certain cancers
- Certain medicines
- Excess vitamin D
- Other metabolic conditions

## Important Interpretation Factors

Total calcium is partly bound to proteins such as albumin.

Albumin levels can therefore affect total calcium interpretation.

In some situations, healthcare professionals may consider ionized calcium.

## Important Limitations

A total calcium result alone cannot determine the cause of an abnormal value.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal calcium result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when significantly abnormal or associated with weakness, confusion, abnormal heart rhythm or other symptoms.

## Related Tests / Context

Albumin, vitamin D, magnesium, phosphorus, creatinine and parathyroid hormone.
