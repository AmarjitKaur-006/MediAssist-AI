# Urine Specific Gravity

## Test Name
Urine Specific Gravity

## Aliases
Specific Gravity, Urine SG

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

Urine specific gravity measures how concentrated or dilute the urine is compared with water.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Specific gravity can provide information about urine concentration and hydration and may help evaluate kidney function or certain fluid-balance conditions.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Very dilute urine can occur with high fluid intake or conditions that affect the kidneys' ability to concentrate urine.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

Concentrated urine may occur with dehydration or increased amounts of certain substances in the urine.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Fluid intake, medicines and medical conditions can affect urine concentration.

## Important Limitations

Specific gravity alone cannot determine the cause of an abnormal result.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An unexpectedly abnormal result should be interpreted with the rest of the urinalysis and the person's clinical context.

## Related Tests / Context

Urine pH, urine protein, glucose and other urine chemistry measurements.
