# Urobilinogen in Urine

## Test Name
Urine Urobilinogen

## Aliases
Urobilinogen, Urine Urobilinogen

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

Urobilinogen is produced when intestinal bacteria break down bilirubin.

The urine test measures the amount of urobilinogen present.

Normal urine contains some urobilinogen.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urobilinogen testing may be part of urinalysis and can provide information related to liver function, bile flow and red blood cell breakdown.

## What Can a Low or Absent Result Mean?

Little or no urobilinogen may occur when bile flow into the intestine is blocked or with certain liver or biliary problems.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

Higher urobilinogen may occur when the liver cannot properly recycle it or when red blood cells are being broken down more rapidly than usual.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Urobilinogen should be interpreted with bilirubin testing and other liver-related findings.

## Important Limitations

Urobilinogen alone cannot diagnose liver disease, bile-duct obstruction or hemolytic anemia.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Abnormal results should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when jaundice, dark urine or other symptoms are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Urine bilirubin, blood bilirubin, ALT, AST and other liver tests.
