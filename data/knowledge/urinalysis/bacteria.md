# Bacteria in Urine

## Test Name
Bacteria in Urine

## Aliases
Urine Bacteria, Bacteriuria

## Category
Urinalysis - Microscopy

## What Does This Test Measure?

Microscopic examination of urine can detect bacteria in a urine sample.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine bacteria may be evaluated when a urinary tract infection is suspected.

## What Can a Negative Result Mean?

No bacteria detected means bacteria were not observed by the method used.

## What Can a Positive Result Mean?

Bacteria in urine may be associated with a urinary tract infection.

However, bacteria may also result from contamination during sample collection.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Bacteria should be interpreted with urine WBCs, nitrites, leukocyte esterase, symptoms and collection quality.

A urine culture may be needed to identify bacteria and guide treatment when infection is suspected.

## Important Limitations

Bacteria seen in a urine sample do not automatically prove that a person has a urinary tract infection.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Discuss significant or persistent bacteriuria with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when symptoms are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Nitrite, leukocyte esterase, urine WBC and urine culture.
