# Urine pH

## Test Name
Urine pH

## Aliases
Urine pH, Urine Acidity

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

Urine pH measures how acidic or alkaline the urine is.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine pH may be measured as part of a urinalysis and can provide information about urine chemistry.

It may be useful when evaluating certain urinary conditions, kidney stones, metabolic conditions or urinary infections.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

More acidic urine can occur with dietary factors, metabolic conditions and other circumstances.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

More alkaline urine can occur with certain diets, some urinary infections and other medical conditions.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Urine pH is affected by diet, medicines, hydration and the time or handling of the urine sample.

## Important Limitations

Urine pH alone cannot diagnose a disease or determine the cause of an abnormal finding.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal urine pH should be interpreted together with the rest of the urinalysis and clinical context.

## Related Tests / Context

Specific gravity, urine protein, glucose, ketones, blood, nitrite and other urinalysis findings.
