# Nitrite in Urine

## Test Name
Urine Nitrite

## Aliases
Nitrites in Urine, Nitrite Test

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

The urine nitrite test checks whether nitrites are present in the urine.

Some bacteria can convert urinary nitrates into nitrites.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Nitrite testing is commonly used as part of urinalysis when a urinary tract infection is suspected.

## What Can a Negative Result Mean?

A negative nitrite result means nitrite was not detected.

A negative result does not completely exclude a urinary tract infection because not all bacteria produce nitrites and other factors can affect the test.

## What Can a Positive Result Mean?

Nitrites in urine may indicate a bacterial urinary tract infection.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Nitrite results should be interpreted together with urine WBCs, leukocyte esterase, symptoms and other tests.

## Important Limitations

A positive nitrite test does not identify the exact bacteria or determine antibiotic sensitivity.

A negative nitrite test does not rule out infection.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

A positive result or urinary symptoms should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

Leukocyte esterase, urine WBC, bacteria and urine culture.
