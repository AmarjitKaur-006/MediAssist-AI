# Urinary Casts

## Test Name
Urinary Casts

## Aliases
Urine Casts, Urinary Casts

## Category
Urinalysis - Microscopy

## What Does This Test Measure?

Urinary casts are tube-shaped structures that can form in the kidney's tubules.

Microscopic examination can identify different types of casts.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urinary casts may provide information about kidney conditions and can be evaluated as part of microscopic urinalysis.

## What Can a Low or Normal Result Mean?

Some types of casts can occur in small amounts, depending on the laboratory's criteria and the person's circumstances.

## What Can an Abnormal Result Mean?

Certain types or increased numbers of casts may be associated with kidney disease or other urinary conditions.

The significance depends heavily on the type of cast found.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Casts must be interpreted according to their type, quantity and the rest of the urinalysis.

## Important Limitations

The presence of a cast alone cannot establish a diagnosis.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Abnormal or significant casts should be interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when other kidney-related findings are abnormal.

## Related Tests / Context

Urine protein, RBC, WBC, bacteria, crystals, creatinine and eGFR.
