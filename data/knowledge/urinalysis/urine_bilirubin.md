# Bilirubin in Urine

## Test Name
Urine Bilirubin

## Aliases
Bilirubin in Urine, Bilirubinuria

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

The urine bilirubin test measures bilirubin in the urine.

Normally, bilirubin is not found in urine.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine bilirubin testing may help evaluate possible liver or bile-duct conditions.

## What Can a Negative Result Mean?

A negative result means bilirubin was not detected.

## What Can a Positive Result Mean?

Bilirubin in urine may occur when bilirubin builds up in the blood and is excreted through the kidneys.

It may be associated with certain liver or bile-duct conditions.

## Important Interpretation Factors

A positive urine bilirubin result should be interpreted with blood bilirubin and other liver tests.

## Important Limitations

Urine bilirubin alone cannot determine the cause of liver or bile-duct dysfunction.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

A positive result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when jaundice or abnormal liver tests are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Total bilirubin, direct bilirubin, ALT, AST, ALP and GGT.
