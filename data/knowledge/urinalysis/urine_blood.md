# Blood in Urine

## Test Name
Urine Blood

## Aliases
Blood in Urine, Hematuria, Occult Blood

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

A urine blood test checks for blood or substances associated with blood in the urine.

A dipstick may detect hemoglobin or myoglobin in addition to intact red blood cells.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine blood testing may help investigate urinary tract, kidney or other conditions.

## What Can a Negative Result Mean?

A negative result means blood-related substances were not detected by the test.

## What Can a Positive Result Mean?

Blood detected in urine may be associated with:

- Urinary tract infection
- Kidney stones
- Kidney disease
- Injury
- Certain urinary tract conditions
- Menstruation or contamination

## Important Interpretation Factors

Microscopic urine examination can help determine whether red blood cells are actually present.

## Important Limitations

A positive dipstick does not by itself identify the source or cause of the blood.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Persistent blood in urine should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

Urine RBC, urine WBC, nitrite, leukocyte esterase and urine microscopy.
