# White Blood Cells in Urine

## Test Name
Urine White Blood Cells

## Aliases
Urine WBC, WBCs in Urine, Leukocytes in Urine

## Category
Urinalysis - Microscopy

## What Does This Test Measure?

The urine WBC test checks for white blood cells in a urine sample.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine WBCs may be evaluated when a urinary tract infection or other urinary or kidney condition is suspected.

## What Can a Normal Result Mean?

Small numbers may occur depending on the laboratory's reference criteria.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

Increased WBCs in urine may be associated with:

- Urinary tract infection
- Kidney infection
- Inflammation of the urinary tract
- Other urinary conditions

## Important Interpretation Factors

Urine WBCs should be interpreted with symptoms and other urinalysis results.

## Important Limitations

WBCs alone do not prove that a bacterial infection is present.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

High or persistent urine WBCs should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when urinary symptoms or fever are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Nitrite, leukocyte esterase, bacteria, urine culture and urine RBC.
