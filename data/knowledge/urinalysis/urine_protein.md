# Urine Protein

## Test Name
Urine Protein

## Aliases
Protein in Urine, Urinary Protein, Proteinuria

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

A urine protein test checks whether protein is present in the urine and, depending on the method, may estimate the amount.

Healthy kidneys normally prevent significant amounts of protein from passing into urine.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine protein testing may help evaluate kidney health and detect possible kidney damage.

## What Can a Low or Negative Result Mean?

A negative or low urine protein result generally indicates that significant protein loss was not detected by the test.

## What Can a Positive or High Result Mean?

Protein in urine may be associated with kidney damage or other conditions.

Temporary increases can also occur with:

- Exercise
- Fever
- Dehydration
- Infection
- Other temporary conditions

## Important Interpretation Factors

A single abnormal result may not indicate persistent kidney disease.

UACR or other quantitative urine tests may be used for more specific evaluation.

## Important Limitations

Dipstick urine protein testing does not provide the same information as a quantitative urine albumin measurement.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Persistent or significant protein in urine should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

UACR, urine albumin, creatinine and eGFR.
