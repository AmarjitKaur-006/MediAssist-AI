# Leukocyte Esterase

## Test Name
Leukocyte Esterase

## Aliases
LE, Leukocyte Esterase in Urine

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

Leukocyte esterase is an enzyme associated with white blood cells.

The urine test checks for leukocyte esterase as an indirect sign that white blood cells may be present.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Leukocyte esterase testing is commonly included in urinalysis when a urinary tract infection is suspected.

## What Can a Negative Result Mean?

A negative result means leukocyte esterase was not detected.

## What Can a Positive Result Mean?

A positive leukocyte esterase result can indicate that white blood cells may be present in the urine.

This can occur with urinary tract inflammation or infection.

## Important Interpretation Factors

The result should be considered together with urine WBCs, nitrites, symptoms and other findings.

## Important Limitations

Leukocyte esterase alone cannot establish that a bacterial urinary tract infection is present.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

A positive result, particularly with urinary symptoms or other abnormal urine findings, should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

Nitrite, urine WBC, bacteria and urine culture.
