# Urine Ketones

## Test Name
Urine Ketones

## Aliases
Ketones in Urine, Ketone Bodies, Ketonuria

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

A urine ketone test measures ketones in the urine.

Ketones are produced when the body breaks down fat for energy instead of using enough glucose.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Ketone testing may be used when evaluating diabetes, prolonged fasting, vomiting, certain metabolic conditions or symptoms suggesting ketoacidosis.

## What Can a Negative Result Mean?

A negative result means significant ketones were not detected by the test.

Small amounts of ketones can sometimes occur under normal circumstances.

## What Can a Positive or High Result Mean?

Higher ketone levels can occur when the body is using more fat for energy.

Possible situations include:

- Fasting
- Prolonged exercise
- Vomiting
- Low-carbohydrate intake
- Uncontrolled diabetes

High ketones may be associated with diabetic ketoacidosis, which can be a medical emergency.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Recent food intake, exercise, illness and diabetes status affect ketone levels.

## Important Limitations

Urine ketones do not directly measure blood ketone levels.

The clinical significance depends on symptoms and other glucose measurements.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

High urine ketones, particularly with high blood glucose, vomiting, abdominal pain, rapid breathing or confusion, warrant prompt medical evaluation.

## Related Tests / Context

Blood glucose, HbA1c and blood ketone testing.
