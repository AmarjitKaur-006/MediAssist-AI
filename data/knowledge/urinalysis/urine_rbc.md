# Red Blood Cells in Urine

## Test Name
Urine Red Blood Cells

## Aliases
Urine RBC, RBCs in Urine, Red Blood Cells in Urine

## Category
Urinalysis - Microscopy

## What Does This Test Measure?

The urine RBC test checks for red blood cells in a urine sample, usually by microscopic examination.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

It may help investigate blood detected by dipstick or symptoms involving the urinary tract or kidneys.

## What Can a Normal Result Mean?

A small number of red blood cells may sometimes be seen depending on the laboratory's reporting criteria.

Interpretation should follow the laboratory's reference range.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

Increased urine RBCs may be associated with:

- Urinary tract infection
- Kidney stones
- Kidney disease
- Urinary tract injury
- Other urinary tract conditions

## Important Interpretation Factors

Results depend on how the sample was collected and examined.

Menstrual blood can contaminate a urine sample.

## Important Limitations

Urine RBCs alone cannot determine the underlying cause of hematuria.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Persistent or significant RBCs in urine should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

Urine blood, WBC, nitrite, leukocyte esterase, bacteria and crystals.
