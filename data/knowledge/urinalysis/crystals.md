# Crystals in Urine

## Test Name
Urine Crystals

## Aliases
Crystals in Urine, Urinary Crystals

## Category
Urinalysis - Microscopy

## What Does This Test Measure?

A urine crystal test checks a urine sample under a microscope for crystals and may identify their type, size and amount.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Crystal testing may help evaluate kidney stones and other urinary or metabolic conditions.

## What Can a Low or Normal Result Mean?

A small number of certain crystals may occur normally.

Interpretation depends on the type and amount of crystals found.

## What Can a High or Abnormal Result Mean?

Large amounts or certain types of crystals may be associated with:

- Kidney stones
- Urinary tract conditions
- Metabolic abnormalities
- Changes in urine concentration or acidity

## Important Interpretation Factors

Crystal formation can be influenced by urine concentration, pH, diet and other factors.

## Important Limitations

The presence of crystals does not automatically mean that kidney stones are present.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Significant or unusual crystals should be interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when pain, blood in urine or recurrent stones are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Urine pH, specific gravity, urine blood, RBC and kidney-related tests.
