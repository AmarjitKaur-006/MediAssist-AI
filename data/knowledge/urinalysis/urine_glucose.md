# Urine Glucose

## Test Name
Urine Glucose

## Aliases
Urine Sugar, Glucosuria, Glycosuria

## Category
Urinalysis

## What Does This Test Measure?

A urine glucose test checks whether glucose is present in the urine.

Normally, most filtered glucose is reabsorbed by the kidneys.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine glucose may be checked as part of a urinalysis and can provide additional information in selected medical situations.

## What Can a Negative Result Mean?

A negative urine glucose result means glucose was not detected by the test.

## What Can a Positive or High Result Mean?

Glucose in urine may occur when blood glucose is elevated or in certain kidney-related conditions.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Urine glucose is not as accurate as blood glucose testing for diagnosing or monitoring diabetes.

## Important Limitations

Urine glucose alone cannot diagnose diabetes.

A blood glucose test is generally more useful when evaluating abnormal glucose metabolism.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Persistent or unexpectedly positive urine glucose should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

Fasting glucose, random glucose and HbA1c.
