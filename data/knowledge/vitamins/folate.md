# Folate

## Test Name
Folate

## Aliases
Folic Acid, Vitamin B9, Folate Blood Test

## Category
Vitamins

## What Does This Test Measure?

Folate is a B vitamin needed for DNA production and normal cell division.

The folate blood test measures the amount of folate in the blood.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Folate testing may be used to evaluate possible folate deficiency, particularly when anemia or nutritional deficiency is suspected.

It may also be relevant when evaluating certain blood-cell abnormalities.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low folate may be associated with:

- Inadequate dietary intake
- Alcohol use
- Problems with intestinal absorption
- Certain medicines
- Increased nutritional requirements

Folate deficiency can contribute to certain forms of anemia.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High folate levels may occur after supplementation or a diet containing substantial amounts of folate.

A high result is generally interpreted in context rather than as a diagnosis by itself.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Folate should be interpreted along with vitamin B12, CBC findings and clinical information when appropriate.

Pregnancy and other situations with increased folate requirements may affect interpretation.

## Important Limitations

Folate alone cannot establish the cause of anemia or another abnormal blood finding.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Discuss abnormal folate results with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when anemia or nutritional concerns are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Vitamin B12, hemoglobin, hematocrit, MCV, MCH and CBC.
