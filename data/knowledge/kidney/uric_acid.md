# Uric Acid

## Test Name
Uric Acid

## Aliases
Serum Uric Acid, Uric Acid Blood Test

## Category
Kidney Function / Metabolism

## What Does This Test Measure?

Uric acid is a waste product produced when the body breaks down purines.

The kidneys normally remove most uric acid from the blood through urine.

The uric acid blood test measures the amount of uric acid in the blood.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Uric acid testing may be used to:

- Help evaluate gout
- Monitor uric acid levels during treatment
- Evaluate some kidney-related conditions
- Investigate conditions associated with abnormal uric acid metabolism

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low uric acid levels are less common and may occur with certain medical conditions, medicines or dietary factors.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High uric acid may be associated with:

- Gout
- Reduced kidney clearance
- Certain medicines
- Increased breakdown of cells
- Dietary factors
- Some metabolic conditions

High uric acid does not necessarily mean that a person has gout.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Uric acid should be interpreted together with symptoms, medical history and other laboratory findings.

Diet, medicines, kidney function and metabolic factors can influence the result.

## Important Limitations

A high uric acid level alone does not diagnose gout.

A person may have elevated uric acid without experiencing gout symptoms.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Discuss persistently abnormal uric acid results with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when joint pain or swelling or other related symptoms are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Creatinine, eGFR, BUN and, when clinically relevant, joint-fluid testing.
