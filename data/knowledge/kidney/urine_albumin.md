# Urine Albumin

## Test Name
Urine Albumin

## Aliases
Urinary Albumin, Albumin in Urine

## Category
Kidney Function

## What Does This Test Measure?

Urine albumin testing measures albumin in the urine.

Healthy kidneys generally keep albumin in the blood rather than allowing significant amounts to pass into urine.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Urine albumin testing may be used to detect kidney damage, particularly in people at increased risk of kidney disease.

It can be especially useful for monitoring people with diabetes or high blood pressure.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

A low or normal urine albumin result generally indicates that little albumin is being lost through the urine.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

Higher urine albumin may indicate kidney damage.

Persistent elevation is more significant than a single abnormal result because temporary increases can occur.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Exercise, fever, infection, dehydration and other temporary conditions may affect urine albumin levels.

Results should be interpreted using the laboratory's method and reference range.

## Important Limitations

One abnormal urine albumin measurement does not automatically establish chronic kidney disease.

Repeat testing may be required.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Persistent elevation in urine albumin should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

UACR, creatinine and eGFR.
