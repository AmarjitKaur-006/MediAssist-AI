# Free Triiodothyronine (Free T3)

## Test Name
Free Triiodothyronine

## Aliases
Free T3, FT3

## Category
Thyroid Function

## What Does This Test Measure?

Free T3 measures the portion of triiodothyronine that is not bound to blood proteins.

T3 is an active thyroid hormone that contributes to regulation of metabolism.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Free T3 may be used with other thyroid tests to evaluate thyroid function, particularly in selected cases of suspected hyperthyroidism.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low free T3 can occur in some thyroid disorders as well as in certain non-thyroid illnesses.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High free T3 may occur with hyperthyroidism.

Interpretation requires comparison with TSH and other thyroid findings.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Free T3 results may be affected by illness, medicines and laboratory methods.

## Important Limitations

Free T3 alone cannot establish a thyroid diagnosis or identify the underlying cause of an abnormal result.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Discuss abnormal free T3 results with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when other thyroid tests are also abnormal or symptoms are present.

## Related Tests / Context

TSH, free T4, total T4 and total T3.
