# Total Triiodothyronine (T3)

## Test Name
Total Triiodothyronine

## Aliases
Total T3, T3, Triiodothyronine

## Category
Thyroid Function

## What Does This Test Measure?

Total T3 measures the total amount of triiodothyronine in the blood, including protein-bound and free hormone.

T3 is one of the thyroid hormones.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

T3 testing may be used when evaluating thyroid function, particularly when hyperthyroidism is suspected.

It may be interpreted together with TSH and T4 measurements.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low total T3 can occur in some thyroid disorders and in certain non-thyroid illnesses.

The result should be interpreted together with other thyroid measurements.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High total T3 may occur in hyperthyroidism and some other thyroid-related conditions.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Total T3 includes protein-bound hormone.

Medicines, pregnancy and other conditions can affect total T3 measurements.

## Important Limitations

Total T3 alone cannot diagnose the cause of abnormal thyroid function.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Discuss abnormal T3 results with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when TSH or T4 results are also abnormal.

## Related Tests / Context

TSH, total T4, free T4 and free T3.
