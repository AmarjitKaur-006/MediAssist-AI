# Total Thyroxine (T4)

## Test Name
Total Thyroxine

## Aliases
Total T4, T4, Thyroxine

## Category
Thyroid Function

## What Does This Test Measure?

Total T4 measures the total amount of thyroxine in the blood, including both protein-bound and free T4.

T4 is a hormone produced by the thyroid gland.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Total T4 may be used to evaluate thyroid function, particularly when interpreted with TSH and other thyroid tests.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low total T4 may be associated with hypothyroidism.

It can also be affected by conditions that change thyroid-binding proteins.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High total T4 may be associated with hyperthyroidism.

Levels can also be affected by changes in thyroid-binding proteins, pregnancy, medicines and other conditions.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Total T4 includes both bound and unbound hormone.

TSH and free T4 often provide important additional context.

## Important Limitations

Total T4 alone cannot establish the cause of abnormal thyroid function.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Discuss an abnormal total T4 result with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when TSH or other thyroid tests are also abnormal.

## Related Tests / Context

TSH, free T4, total T3 and free T3.
