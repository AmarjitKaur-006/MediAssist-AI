# Free Thyroxine (Free T4)

## Test Name
Free Thyroxine

## Aliases
Free T4, FT4

## Category
Thyroid Function

## What Does This Test Measure?

Free T4 measures the amount of thyroxine that is not bound to blood proteins.

Free T4 represents the portion available to tissues.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

Free T4 is commonly used with TSH to evaluate thyroid function.

It may help evaluate possible hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism and monitor treatment.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

A low free T4 may be associated with hypothyroidism.

The pattern of TSH and free T4 results helps provide interpretation.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

A high free T4 may be associated with hyperthyroidism.

Certain medicines, illness and other factors can also affect thyroid hormone measurements.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Free T4 should usually be interpreted together with TSH.

Pregnancy, medicines and laboratory methods can affect interpretation.

## Important Limitations

Free T4 alone cannot establish the exact cause of abnormal thyroid function.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Discuss abnormal free T4 results with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when TSH is also abnormal.

## Related Tests / Context

TSH, total T4, total T3 and free T3.
