# Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT)

## Test Name
Oral Glucose Tolerance Test

## Aliases
OGTT, Glucose Tolerance Test, Oral Glucose Tolerance

## Category
Blood Glucose / Diabetes

## What Does This Test Measure?

The oral glucose tolerance test measures how the body processes glucose over a period of time after a person drinks a glucose-containing solution.

Blood samples are collected at specified times.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

The test may be used to:

- Evaluate glucose regulation
- Help diagnose diabetes
- Evaluate gestational diabetes in pregnancy
- Investigate abnormal glucose results in selected situations

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

An unexpectedly low glucose level during testing may indicate hypoglycemia or altered glucose regulation.

Interpretation depends on timing and the clinical context.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

Higher-than-expected glucose levels during the test may indicate impaired glucose regulation or diabetes depending on the testing protocol and applicable diagnostic criteria.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Interpretation depends on:

- Fasting preparation
- Timing of blood samples
- Amount of glucose administered
- Whether the test is being performed for pregnancy or another indication

## Important Limitations

OGTT results should be interpreted using the specific protocol and criteria used by the healthcare facility.

One result should not be interpreted without considering the patient's clinical context.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

Abnormal OGTT results should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Related Tests / Context

Fasting glucose, random glucose and HbA1c.
