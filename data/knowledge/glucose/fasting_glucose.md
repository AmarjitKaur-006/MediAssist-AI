# Fasting Blood Glucose

## Test Name
Fasting Blood Glucose

## Aliases
Fasting Glucose, FPG, FBS, Fasting Blood Sugar

## Category
Blood Glucose / Diabetes

## What Does This Test Measure?

A fasting blood glucose test measures the amount of glucose in the blood after a person has not eaten for a specified period of time.

Glucose is the body's main source of energy.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

The test may be used to:

- Screen for diabetes or prediabetes
- Help diagnose diabetes
- Monitor blood glucose in people with diabetes

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

Low blood glucose may occur when blood sugar falls below the expected range.

Possible contributing factors include:

- Diabetes medicines
- Not eating enough
- Increased physical activity
- Certain medical conditions

The cause depends on the individual context.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

High fasting glucose may be associated with:

- Prediabetes
- Diabetes
- Certain medicines
- Stress or illness
- Other conditions affecting glucose metabolism

An elevated fasting glucose result does not by itself establish the cause.

## Important Interpretation Factors

The result depends on appropriate fasting before the test.

Interpretation should follow the laboratory's reference or diagnostic criteria and the patient's clinical context.

## Important Limitations

A single abnormal glucose result may not be sufficient for diagnosis.

Healthcare professionals may use repeat testing or another diabetes test to confirm an abnormal result.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal fasting glucose result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when persistent or accompanied by symptoms.

## Related Tests / Context

HbA1c, random glucose and oral glucose tolerance testing.
