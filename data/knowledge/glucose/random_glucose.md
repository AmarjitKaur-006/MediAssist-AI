# Random Blood Glucose

## Test Name
Random Blood Glucose

## Aliases
Random Glucose, Random Blood Sugar, RBS

## Category
Blood Glucose / Diabetes

## What Does This Test Measure?

A random blood glucose test measures the amount of glucose in the blood at a particular time without requiring fasting.

## Why Is This Test Performed?

It may be used to:

- Evaluate blood glucose when fasting is not required
- Investigate symptoms of high or low blood glucose
- Help evaluate possible diabetes
- Monitor glucose in some clinical situations

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

A low random glucose result may indicate low blood glucose.

Possible causes include diabetes medicines, inadequate food intake, increased physical activity and certain medical conditions.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

A high random glucose level may occur with:

- Diabetes
- Stress or acute illness
- Certain medicines
- Other metabolic conditions

Interpretation depends partly on when and what the person recently ate.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Because the person does not need to fast, recent food and drink can affect the result.

Symptoms and the timing of the test should be considered.

## Important Limitations

A random glucose result alone may not be sufficient to diagnose or exclude diabetes.

Additional testing may be required.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An abnormal random glucose result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional, especially when symptoms or repeated abnormal results are present.

## Related Tests / Context

Fasting glucose, HbA1c and oral glucose tolerance testing.
