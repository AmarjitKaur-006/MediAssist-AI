# Non-HDL Cholesterol

## Test Name
Non-HDL Cholesterol

## Aliases
Non-HDL-C, Non-HDL Cholesterol

## Category
Lipid Profile

## What Does This Test Measure?

Non-HDL cholesterol represents the amount of cholesterol carried by lipoproteins other than HDL.

It can be calculated from total cholesterol and HDL cholesterol.

## Why Is This Test Used?

Non-HDL cholesterol may be used as another measure of cholesterol-related cardiovascular risk.

It can provide information about the cholesterol carried by several potentially atherogenic lipoproteins.

## What Can a Low Result Mean?

A lower non-HDL cholesterol value is generally associated with lower cholesterol-related cardiovascular risk.

## What Can a High Result Mean?

A high non-HDL cholesterol level may be associated with increased cardiovascular risk.

## Important Interpretation Factors

Non-HDL cholesterol should be considered together with LDL, HDL, triglycerides and the patient's overall cardiovascular risk.

The appropriate treatment target depends on individual clinical circumstances.

## Important Limitations

Non-HDL cholesterol does not provide a complete assessment of cardiovascular risk by itself.

## When Should the Result Be Discussed With a Healthcare Professional?

An elevated non-HDL cholesterol result should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional in the context of the person's complete cardiovascular risk profile.

## Related Tests / Context

Total cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, HDL cholesterol and triglycerides.
